Overexposed Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Overexposed is a free single-player game supported by ads. The game itself has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other personal details.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. On iOS you choose whether apps may track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change their choice later.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data stored on your device

Settings and play time are kept only on your device and are removed when you delete the game. We do not run our own servers and do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
