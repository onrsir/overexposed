Overexposed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Overexposed is a single-player offline game. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

The game does not save personal data. Anything the game keeps (such as settings) stays only on your device and is removed when you delete the game.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
